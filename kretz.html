Kretz: finance and operations for sports clubs

A digital finance and operations platform for sports clubs, with an app and a website. Everything the club needs, in one place.

Where it started

I have spent ten years in sports: six as a player, six as a coach and two on a club board as deputy leader responsible for the finances. Running a club's economy meant juggling spreadsheets, separate payment tools and a lot of manual follow-up done by volunteers.

Validating the problem

Before building, I surveyed sports clubs across Midt-Norge.

700clubs surveyed
249responses
58interested
14want to pilot

A Trondheim club with more than 400 members has also offered to help build the platform.

Funding and next steps

Kretz has received support through SMN-bidraget via Spark* NTNU. The pitch covered the problem, today's solutions, our solution, the market, strategy and a budget with a timeline. I'm now following up four pilot clubs to understand what they need next.

What it shows

Kretz is where my finance background meets product work: understanding how money moves through an organisation, then designing software that makes it simpler for the people who run it.
