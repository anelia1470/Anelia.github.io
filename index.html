    Python

    pandas, statsmodels, scikit-learn and LLM tooling for retrieval and evaluation.

    # Share of evidence found in top k
def recall_at_k(retrieved, gold, k=5):
    top = set(retrieved[:k])
    return len(top & gold) / len(gold)

  AVG(ret) OVER (
    PARTITION BY permno
    ORDER BY month
