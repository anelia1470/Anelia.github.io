Financial news moves fast, and most of it is forgotten a week later. Writing about it is how I make sure I've actually understood it.

This blog is where I comment on what's happening in the markets: rate decisions, earnings, deals, and more and more often, what AI is doing to the finance industry itself.

## How each post works

Every note follows the same three steps:

1. **What happened.** The news in a few sentences, with a link to the source.
2. **Why it matters.** The finance behind it: valuation, risk, incentives, or the theory that explains it.
3. **What I'd watch next.** The number, decision or signal that will show whether the story holds up.
